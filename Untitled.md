 A multi-tenant real-time location system (RTLS) for industrial sites — warehouses, yards, and  manufacturing floors. The platform tracks physical assets using IoT hardware and continuously answers where an asset is, whether its state violates defined rules, and who needs to be alerted. Supports multiple tracking technologies including BLE, LoRaWAN GPS trackers, and RFID readers, all normalized through a unified ingestion pipeline
